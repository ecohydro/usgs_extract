### Summary of the Document

The document provides historical monthly discharge data in second-feet for two locations within the San Joaquin River Basin in California: near Three Rivers on the Kaweah River and at McKay Point, near Lemon Cove.

1. **Kaweah River Near Three Rivers**:
   - The drainage area is 520 square miles.
   - Data spans several years from 1902-03 to 1926-27.
   - Monthly discharge values are recorded for each year across the months October through September of the following year.
   - The average monthly discharges over the observed period are also provided.

2. **Kaweah River at McKay Point, Near Lemon Cove**:
   - Data is available from 1918-19 to 1920-21.
   - Similar monthly discharge values are recorded with some years having missing data points.
